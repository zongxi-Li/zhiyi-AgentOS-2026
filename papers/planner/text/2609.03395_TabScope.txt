E     Scope Selection Analysis
E.1    Oracle Scope Selection
The oracle results in Table 11 indicate clear room
for improvement in scope selection. On WikiTQ, or-
acle selection improves EM from 82.4% to 87.8%,
giving a 5.4 point gain over the better fixed branch.
On SLQA, the corresponding gain is 3.0 points, from
72.3% to 75.3%. Since Full and Local achieve
similar performance on both datasets, the results
suggest that the main benefit comes from selecting
the appropriate scope for each instance rather than
relying on a single fixed scope.

Setting. We runs use GPT-5-mini as the base
