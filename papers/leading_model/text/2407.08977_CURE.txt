Accuracy via calibrated noise-injection. To estimate accuracy

deviation and Lattigo-reported CKKS noise growth. This is per-
formed on Colab T4 [20] for fast iteration. We ensure the activa-
tion approximation and quantization match the Go/HE pipeline.
