                                         more challenging. Specifically, (i) deploying LLMs on devices
                                         faces computational, memory, and energy resource issues, while
                                         (ii) deploying them in the cloud cannot guarantee real-time
                                         service and incurs communication/usage costs. In this paper,
                                         we design TMO, a device-cloud LLM inference system with
